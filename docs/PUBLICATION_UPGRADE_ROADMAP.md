# PGAA Publication Upgrade Roadmap

Date: 2026-08-02

## Purpose

This note reduces the current project state to the smallest set of upgrade moves that would actually raise the work one publication level.
It is not a generic wish list. It is a route-selection document based on the current evidence tree, route audit, and claim ceilings.

## Current route decision

The current recommended route is `stable_resistance_state_bifurcation`.
That is the strongest immediate publication path because it already has:

- held-out DTEC reproduction of the branch axis;
- prospective GSE193258 branch assignment;
- independent GSE335846 dynamic calibration support;
- a locked module route with explicit failure handling;
- a clear way to keep claim ceilings below wet-lab overreach.

The `failure_preserving_benchmark_engine` route remains valuable, but it is the secondary route if the submission is framed as a method paper rather than a biological decision paper.
The immune/event-peptide route is not the mainline unless a matched event-plus-expression source is added and the decoy-guarded tier collapse is resolved.

## What currently raises the work above a routine score paper

1. It does not collapse results into one ranking.
2. It preserves blocked, provisional, and failed states.
3. It makes the route decision explicit instead of hiding it in Discussion prose.
4. It separates computational support from biological validation.
5. It already has a concrete next experiment, rather than a vague future-work paragraph.

That is enough to justify a serious methods-plus-application paper, but not yet enough for an overclaimed biological discovery paper.

## Best publication-level upgrade direction

The best way to move the work up one level is to strengthen the `stable_resistance_state_bifurcation` route, not to broaden the manuscript into unrelated validation themes.

The reason is simple:

- this route already has a coherent biological object;
- it has multiple independent checkpoints;
- it supports a predeclared next decisive action;
- it can be written as a decision paper with a bounded claim ceiling.

By contrast, the immune/event-peptide route currently reads as a separate infrastructure track.
It is useful, but it would split attention and dilute the manuscript unless matched event-expression evidence is added.

## Concrete upgrade priorities

### Priority 1

Obtain source numerical tables for `GSE335846`, or run a targeted prospective ATM dose-response across branch-weak and branch-strong DTP states.

Why this matters:

- it upgrades the current dynamic support from indirect calibration to stronger source-level evidence;
- it turns the branch axis into a more defensible decision object;
- it makes the result easier for reviewers to audit.

### Priority 2

Evaluate HDAC inhibition as the strongest secondary interaction, but keep it secondary.

Why this matters:

- the current evidence already suggests HDAC is a follow-up candidate;
- the paper gets weaker if the mainline is blurred by a second co-primary narrative;
- a secondary branch keeps the main claim clean.

### Priority 3

Add a third independent PC9 evolution system for the locked resistance module route.

Why this matters:

- the current down-module route is promising but still limited to two clone-level systems;
- a third system would materially improve credibility;
- it would move the route from a development gate toward a more submission-stable biological story.

### Priority 4

Keep the claim-state compiler and failure-preserving benchmark engine as the governance layer, not the headline biology.

Why this matters:

- it protects the manuscript from evidentiary inflation;
- it preserves negative and blocked states;
- it gives the reviewer a visible audit surface without hijacking the central biological story.

### Priority 5

Keep the immune/event-peptide route as a separate future track unless matched event-expression evidence appears.

Why this matters:

- the current evidence is public-domain transfer evidence, not matched biological validation;
- merging it into the main story would force the claim ceiling upward without the evidence to support it;
- this is the fastest way to turn a strong computational paper into an overclaimed one.

## What to avoid

- Do not reposition the paper as an immune-validation paper unless direct event, ligand, and T-cell evidence are all in hand.
- Do not make HDAC co-primary unless independent support is obtained.
- Do not let the benchmark-governance layer become the only story; it is necessary but not sufficient for a stronger submission.
- Do not use bounded computational concordance as a substitute for wet-lab validation.

## Submission-ready shape

The strongest realistic submission shape is:

1. a biologically coherent resistance-state decision object;
2. a locked route with explicit failure-preserving governance;
3. one or two independent external calibration systems;
4. a clearly stated next decisive experiment;
5. no unsupported validation language.

That is a publishable manuscript shape.
It is not yet a discovery paper with full experimental closure, and it should not be written as one.

## Final recommendation

If the goal is to move the work up one publication level, the next investment should go into:

1. source-level reinforcement of the branch axis,
2. a third-system lock for the down-module route,
3. tighter separation between mainline biology and secondary validation tracks.

That combination is more likely to produce a submission-grade paper than expanding the scope into weaker auxiliary routes.
