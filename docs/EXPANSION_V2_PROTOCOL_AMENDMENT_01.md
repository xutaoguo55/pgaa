# Expansion v2 Protocol Amendment 01

This amendment was frozen after metadata-only contract compilation and before any expansion-v2 expression-derived score, overlap, margin, or claim state was generated.

McFarland et al. is a pooled multi-lineage cancer drug-response experiment. No individual cell line contains the frozen minimum of eight eligible drug units, but the study contains many eligible cell-line-by-drug events observed at both 6 h and 24 h with matched controls. The platform is therefore represented as one source study with `cell line x drug x dose` analysis units. It still counts once in the ten-platform estimand and once as the `multi_lineage_cancer_cell_line_panel` context class.

Only cells annotated as normal quality, drug perturbation type, and recorded time 6 h or 24 h are eligible. Controls are matched within cell line and analysis split. Unit selection remains descending minimum equal group size followed by unit identifier, with the frozen maximum of 16 units.

No method, threshold, multiplicity correction, seed, primary estimand, or claim-promotion rule changes. The machine-readable amendment is `evidence/expansion_v2_protocol_amendment_01.json`.
